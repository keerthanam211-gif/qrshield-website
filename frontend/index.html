Methodology

How QRShield decides

No default verdicts. Every result comes from real checks run when you scan.

1 · Decode

QR codes are decoded on your device (native BarcodeDetector where available, jsQR otherwise). The decoded text appears instantly.

2 · Trace

Links are followed server-side through every HTTP, meta-refresh and script redirect to reveal the real destination — without exposing your device.

3 · Check intelligence

Every URL in the chain is checked against live threat intelligence, plus domain registration age (RDAP).

4 · Analyse patterns

Brand impersonation, typosquatting, look-alike characters, APK downloads, USSD codes and UPI scam patterns raise the risk score.

UNVERIFIEDThe security check could not be completed (backend or intelligence services unreachable, destination unreachable, or content that can't be verified).

UPI payment safety

  • Scanning a UPI QR and entering your PIN always sends money. You never need to scan to receive a refund, cashback or prize.
  • Always match the payee name shown by your UPI app with who you intend to pay.
  • Banks, KYC desks and customer care never ask you to pay via QR.
  • Report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (India).
